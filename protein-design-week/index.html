what life
encoded is

Life Encoded is a European AI–Bio Week: 7 days in Lausanne, on the EPFL campus, bringing together the researchers, builders and entrepreneurs working where machine learning meets bioengineering.

The week runs a 48-hour hackathon, with multiple working tracks on the field's open problems, and an ecosystem summit with two round tables connecting labs, startups and industry.

Europe has the science and the talent for this field. What it lacks is a room where all of it shows up at once — Life Encoded is that room.

who
we are

Life Encoded is organised by three student associations working at the intersection of AI and biology.

DLAI
12projects
40students
8partner labs

Designing Life with AI is an engineering project where students design, model, and experimentally test novel proteins using computational tools and wet-lab methods.

SV Industry
7events / year
50–100participants / event

The mission of SV Industry is to bridge the gap between Life Sciences Engineering students and industry actors. We organise conferences, workshops and roundtables throughout the year.

BEAM
6universities
4countries
40+student partners

BioEngineering and Machine Learning Europe is a non-profit association connecting Europe's emerging talent in AI applied to biology. Founded in 2026 across EPFL, TUM and Sorbonne Université, BEAM runs from 6 technical institutions in 4 countries. Life Encoded is its first European week.
